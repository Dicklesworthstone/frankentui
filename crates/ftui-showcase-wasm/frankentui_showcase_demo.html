FrankenTUI demo application. Use Tab and Shift+Tab to navigate between screens, or swipe in from the left or right edge on a touch screen. Shift+drag selects text and Ctrl/Cmd+C copies it; Ctrl/Cmd+V pastes. Press ? for help. On a touch screen the bar along the bottom has a button for every key the current screen uses, press and hold on the terminal to drag, and a divider can be dragged straight away.
